# Project Report Requirements
Please prepare your project report using the IEEE format. Refer to the attached formatting requirements and example publications.

- Limits: Maximum 6 pages
- References are excluded from the page limit.
The example publications provided may be longer than the page limits listed above. However, your project report must not exceed the required page limit. Reports exceeding the page limit will not be reviewed or graded.

The example publications are provided only as references for formatting, organization, and presentation of results. You are not expected to produce work at the same level or scope as a published journal or conference paper.
